ReciPies should be able to convert measurements from one another. Like dl to cups, tablespoons to ounces, etc.

This should be done by unifying on a single unit per type of measurement.

For example 1 dl equals 100 ml. 1 cup equals 240 ml. The unifying measurement for volume units should thus be ml.
A unifying measurement should exist for every type of unit (volume, weight, others?).

Volume units, unified by ml:
1 cl: 10 ml
1 dl: 100 ml
1 l: 1000 ml
1 cup: 240 ml


Weight units, unified by g:
1 kg: 1000 g
1 pound: 454 g
1 oz: 28,35 g
